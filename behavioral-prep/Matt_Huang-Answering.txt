1. What is a Behavioral Interview
- A question to demonstrate a certain quality

2. Qualities
- Leadership
    Interviewers want someone that will climb the latter.
    Closer to someone that will take the Initiative.
- Resilience
    How do you react to challenge and failure.
    Interviewers are interested in the journey, not the destination.
- Teamwork
    Interviewers want someone that can work in a team because you will be working in a team.
- Influencing / Persuasion
    Interviewers are looking for someone who can communicate well
- Ethical / Moral Conflict
    Will test your integrity
    Tricky: dont want to be a snitch, but also dont want to turn a blind eye 

3. Step 1: Brain Dump
- Create a record of all experiences you have with each quality(s)

4. Step 2: Craft your arsenal
- Use the STAR method to create a cohesive narrative.
    Situation: the context
    Task: What you are asked to do?
    Action: What you did.
    Result: What was the outcome of your action
- Add "and what i learned from that experience ..."
    This ties together the qualities
- Show dont Tell
    BAD - "My boss told me i messed up and i felt upset"
    GOOD - "My boss told me i messed up and my first thought was boy am i screwed"
    Understand that interviewers go through 100s of applicants, tell stories to
    keep them interested
- Have 1-2 stories per quality

5. Practice Delivery
- How to avoid sounding "scripted"
    Dont write what to say, write what to cover (bullet points)
    Record Yourself, you are your best critique
    Remove Filler words, pauses > like, um, ya know

6. Examples by quality
- Really explain the situation dont just say this is what happened. provide details
- Leadership & Influencing
    Convinced senior to renew subsciption using suvery of students, learned that data is a major persuasion point
- Resilience / Success
    Wanted to play violin at big internation, auditioned, but failed, was upset, but got a callback for different instrument
    Practiced viola so much (practiced so much even on vacation), got it a went all over the world.
    Learned the opportunities may come in different forms
- Ethical / Moral Ethics
    worked at UPS, hard work (show), noticed co-worker stealing hours, at the time didnt see problem, However realized after talking
    with brother that its the little things that reflect us, so broght it to manager, and they implemented a better time management system.
    Learned that the little things that people dont see that define us
