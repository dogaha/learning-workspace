1. Why the 2-minute story?
- 1st impression / sets the tone
    The first time you will be talking, first impression of who you are.
    People create a opinion based on the first 30 seconds.
    People focus on begining and end.
    Sets the tone, if you are awkward the interview will be awkward 
- This will show up 99% of interviews and networking
    What do you Want
    Why are you here
    Who are you

2. Part 1: Content
- Keep under 2 minutes, just a sample
- have a logical flow, make it a story
- Keep the story high leve, but include details to keep it interesting
- End with how you are excited to be here
    Dont leave interviewer not knowing why you are here
    Dont end it unenthusiatically like "so yeah, thats it..."
- Sample Story Format
    Origin (location) and Education ~ 10seconds
    Experience 1
    Experience 2
    Today

    Pivot = how the experience lead to the next
    EX: you did a project, you enjoyed a specific aspect, and lead to a new role

3. Part 2: Delivery
- Smile, be human. Your interviewer is also human, dont treat them as a "god"
- Speak like you work there
    Confidence, speak like you deserve to be there
    Lingo, undestand how people in the industry talk
- "dial up" the energy
    appear excited
    you are coming in with conviction, that you are qualified
    Your interviewer has low conviction of you.
    you job is to make them believe that you can do it.
- Actionable steps
    Record yourself.
